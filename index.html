Timeline, latest first

  • News21 Oct 2026 (The News Minute)
    Heavy rain since Tuesday night (20 Oct) flooded T Nagar, Velachery, Nungambakkam, Ashok Nagar, West Mambalam, Alwarpet, Kilpauk and Kolathur, and GN Chetty Road. GCC used over 100 motor pumps plus tractors and JCBs across its 15 zones. An official said some spots needed 3–4 more hours to drain.
  • GCC statement21 Oct 2026, Wednesday morning (Deccan Herald)
    Commissioner said Jones, Perambur and Gengu Reddy subways flooded overnight, were drained and reopened. All 420 bus-route roads open. Pumping continued at AGS Colony, Velachery. City average rain was 7.3 cm in 24 hours.
  • News21 Oct 2026 (Bombay Samachar)
    Schools shut in five districts. Meenambakkam got 120.3 mm and Nungambakkam 107.2 mm in the 21 hours to 5:30 AM. No major citywide inundation reported; 200 relief centres ready.
  • @ChennaiTraffic21 Oct 2026, morning
    Traffic diversions 7:30–9:00 AM for the Police Commemoration Day Parade at the DGP Office, Mylapore. Not flood-related, but affects Mylapore roads.
  • @ChennaiTrafficDate not shown on profile; listed just after the 21 Oct post (Tamil)
    Yanaikavani traffic police found and cleared a blocked stormwater drain on Yanaikavani Street during heavy rain, restoring traffic flow.
  • News19 Oct 2026 (DT Next, about Sunday 18 Oct)
    Vyasarpadi: Stephenson Road had near knee-deep water after overnight rain. Residents say Ganesapuram Subway floods every year and water is still pumped out manually; about 15 m of drain work is pending.
  • GCC statement18 Oct 2026, 5:23 PM IST (PTI)
    All 22 subways cleared of waterlogging and reopened by 6 AM Sunday. Some areas got over 30 cm of rain; no casualties or major accidents reported.
  • News18 Oct 2026 (Deccan Chronicle)
    Water cleared from all 35 inundated locations using motor pumps. Food given to 13,500 people. 1,189 vehicles and machines on standby, including 676 corporation pumps and 134 rented 100 HP pumps.
  • News18 Oct 2026 (DT Next)
    Severe waterlogging inspected at Parrys, Broadway Bus Terminus, Triplicane and Ice House. About 15,000 workers deployed; 50-member TNDRF battalion on standby at Ripon Building.
  • News17 Oct 2026 (DT Next)
    Teams cleared silt from catch pits and drains on Besant Road, Teynampet, a recurring waterlogging spot.
